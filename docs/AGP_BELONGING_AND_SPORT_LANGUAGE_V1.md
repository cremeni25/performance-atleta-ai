# AGP BELONGING & SPORT LANGUAGE V1

## Status
CANONICAL / FROZEN PRINCIPLE

This document defines a product property of AGP, not a marketing layer.

## Core identity
AGP is not a commercial back-office system that happens to serve sport.
AGP must operate as part of the daily sporting environment of the people involved in athlete development.

The experience must create belonging from first contact through the full longitudinal relationship.

For Pool Swimming, the user must feel inside swimming from the first screen to the last interaction.
The same principle will apply to every future Sport Domain Profile.

## Canonical rule
One AGP Core. Multiple deeply specialized sport experiences.

Core concepts remain stable and language-neutral.
Visible terminology, workflows, evidence, metrics, roles, competition structure and next actions are resolved by:

sport + modality + locale + role + project + person + longitudinal context.

## Belonging from start to finish
Belonging is a functional requirement.

The system must not open with generic corporate language and later become sport-specific.
The sport identity must be present at first access, daily use, analysis, decision, intervention, reports and longitudinal history.

For Swimming, examples include:
- Swimmer / Nadador rather than a generic participant label when sport context is known.
- Head Swim Coach / Técnico de Natação rather than a generic professional-group label.
- Training language native to swimming: set, repetition, split, pace, stroke, start, turn, underwater, breakout, event, pool course, dry-land.
- Role-aware vocabulary for coach, athlete, analyst and specialists.

## Interpersonal language
AGP must use the language people in that sport naturally use with each other.

This does not permit slang to replace scientific precision.
The system separates:
- canonical concept;
- scientific definition;
- localized sport terminology;
- professional interpretation.

The same concept may have different visible wording by country/region without changing scientific meaning.

## Individual and collective context
The individual athlete is the longitudinal center.
The team, technical staff, specialists and institution provide context, collaboration and governance.

AGP must preserve the individual history when team, coach, category, project, season, event or institution changes.
Collective intelligence must not erase individual development.

## 24-hour longitudinal operating concept
AGP is not restricted to the training session or competition.
It follows the athlete's development continuously through authorized evidence and context across the day, week, microcycle, mesocycle, season and years.

24-hour accompaniment means continuity of context, not invasive surveillance.
AGP only uses authorized evidence and does not imply continuous personal tracking where no lawful/authorized data source exists.

Relevant dimensions may include training, competition, recovery, sleep, readiness, health, growth, maturation, psychology, nutrition, interventions and responses according to scope and professional competence.

## Next-action experience
Every role should preferentially see:
1. what requires attention now;
2. why it requires attention;
3. what evidence supports that state;
4. what action that role is authorized to take;
5. what happens next in the longitudinal cycle.

The user should not need to navigate a generic collection of modules to discover their work.

## Role principle
One person may hold multiple roles simultaneously.

Canonical access derives from:
identity + role + institution/project scope + professional credential/competence + sport context.

"Technical Commission" is an organizational grouping, not a generic user identity.
AGP must distinguish Technical Team from Specialist / Multidisciplinary Professionals.

## Master principle
Master is platform governance, not the routine operator of coach, athlete or specialist work.
Master may supervise, configure and homologate but must not be used as the permanent operational substitute for the real roles.

## Global benchmark principle
TRITONWEAR, CATAPULT, VALD, KITMAN LABS and ATHLETE MONITORING remain recurring benchmark references.

AGP uses them to identify:
- capabilities that work in real high-performance environments;
- workflow and integration patterns worth preserving;
- known friction, data overload and interpretation gaps;
- opportunities where evidence + context + competence + intervention + response + longitudinal learning can be better integrated.

They are references, not templates to copy.

## World-standard ambition
The target is not feature count.
The target is a coherent world-standard operating environment that can answer:

Given this athlete, in this sport, at this point in development, with these authorized data collected through these protocols and this evidence quality:
- what do we know?
- what do we not know?
- what changed?
- what deserves attention?
- who has competence to act?
- what is the next justified action?
- what happened after the intervention?

## Swimming-first, multi-sport-ready
Pool Swimming is the first complete Sport Domain Profile.

The system must be built deeply for swimming now while keeping the Core extensible for future sports.
Future sport expansion must normally require a new Sport Domain Profile and locale/terminology packages, not reconstruction of identity, evidence, longitudinal cycle, governance or core authorization architecture.

## Product boundary
AGP must never regress into:
- a generic form system;
- a generic score dashboard;
- a commercial CRM-style interface;
- one vocabulary for every sport;
- a single generic professional profile;
- a data warehouse without a justified next action.

## Point Zero
Professional real-world homologation happens when the AGP reaches Point Zero: a coherent, usable, end-to-end system ready to begin real operation.

Scientific traceability is required during construction.
External professional validation and operational correction occur at Point Zero and during controlled real use; they do not block completion of the product architecture before a usable system exists.
