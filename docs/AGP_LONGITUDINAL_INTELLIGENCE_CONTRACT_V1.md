# AGP LONGITUDINAL INTELLIGENCE CONTRACT V1

## Status
CANONICAL / FROZEN PRINCIPLE

## Purpose
The AGP longitudinal layer is not a historical archive and not a generic trend chart.
It is the continuity mechanism that connects the athlete across day, microcycle, mesocycle, season and career while preserving context changes.

## Canonical temporal hierarchy
career -> season -> mesocycle -> microcycle -> day

A child cycle belongs to the same person as its parent. Institution, project, coach, category, event, team and even Sport Domain Profile may change over a career without resetting the person history.

## Individual center
The person/athlete is the longitudinal center. Project and institution are contexts, not the owner of the athlete's historical identity.

## Operational continuity
Training sessions, competitions, collections/evidence, interventions and responses must be linkable to the appropriate temporal cycle.
The system must retain what was planned, what actually happened, what evidence was available, what decision/intervention occurred and what response followed.

## Evidence before interpretation
The longitudinal engine must never turn chronological proximity into causality.
A temporal sequence can support observation of association or response; causal conclusions require appropriate evidence design and professional review.

## Comparison contract
Before comparing observations across time, the AGP must evaluate comparability, including where relevant:
- metric canonical identity and scientific version;
- unit;
- protocol/version;
- measurement context;
- device/instrument/source;
- data quality and completeness;
- athlete development stage;
- sport/event/stroke context;
- training/competition context.

When comparability is insufficient, the correct state is `dados_insuficientes` or `nao_comparavel`, not an invented trend.

## What the longitudinal layer may produce
At the structural stage it may produce traceable facts such as:
- observations ordered in time;
- coverage by cycle;
- planned versus executed evidence;
- presence/absence of intervention and response;
- changes in context;
- metric series under comparable conditions.

It must not automatically produce a global performance score, diagnosis, injury prediction or universal ranking.

## 24-hour concept
24-hour accompaniment means continuity of authorized context across the athlete's day and development, not continuous invasive surveillance.
No absence of data may be silently converted into a negative athlete state.

## Multi-sport readiness
This temporal core is sport-agnostic. Pool Swimming is the first Sport Domain Profile using it deeply.
Future sports should reuse identity, cycles, evidence, intervention/response and governance while adding their own domain profile, terminology and applicability rules.
