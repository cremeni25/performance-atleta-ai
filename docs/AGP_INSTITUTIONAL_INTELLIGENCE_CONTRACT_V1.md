# AGP INSTITUTIONAL INTELLIGENCE CONTRACT V1

## Status
CANONICAL / FROZEN PRINCIPLE

Institutional intelligence exists to organize context, attention, resources and continuity around individual development. It must never erase the athlete as the longitudinal center.

## Core rule
AGP institutional intelligence is derived from authorized individual longitudinal states, operational coverage, project context and evidence quality.

It is not a leaderboard.
It is not a universal team score.
It is not a ranking of athletes.

## What the institution may know
Within authorized scope, the institutional layer may aggregate:
- active people and role coverage;
- projects and sport contexts;
- evidence coverage;
- longitudinal evidence maturity;
- sessions and competitions;
- protocol/comparability quality issues;
- open operational work;
- intervention/response coverage when available;
- contextual trends only when methodological equivalence is valid.

## What the institution may not infer automatically
The institutional layer must not infer that:
- less evidence means worse performance;
- a higher or lower arbitrary global score means better or worse athlete;
- one athlete is superior to another because heterogeneous metrics differ;
- a team average represents every individual;
- temporal association proves coaching, treatment or institutional causality.

## Individual sovereignty inside collective intelligence
Every aggregate must remain traceable to authorized underlying evidence, while the final product preserves privacy and role scope.

Collective intelligence must answer questions such as:
- Where is evidence missing?
- Which contexts need professional attention?
- Which projects are longitudinally observable?
- Where is comparability broken?
- Which operational processes are open?
- Where is resource or specialist coverage insufficient?

It must not answer “who is the best athlete?” using a synthetic AGP score.

## Role and scope rule
Institution managers may access institutional aggregates according to `institution.aggregate_read`.
Technical roles may access project/team aggregates only in projects to which they are actively linked and only within effective capabilities.
Master governance may supervise platform-wide structure but does not replace routine professional operation.

## Product principle
Institutional intelligence must reduce management complexity while increasing scientific and operational depth underneath.

This follows the AGP product rule:

> The greater the intelligence underneath, the less complexity should be exposed to the user.

## Legacy boundary
Legacy score-based institutional diagnostics and athlete rankings are non-canonical experimental residue. They must not be used as the decision model of AGP Point 0.
